# Ilayisensi

**The Selah isiXhosa Rendering** (inguqulelo yesiXhosa yeSelah) —
inguqulelo yeprojekthi yeSelah yeTanakh yesiHebhere ngesiXhosa,
ethembekileyo koonobumba (iinguqulelo zendinyana nganye neenkcazelo
zethokheni nganye), kunye nembali yohlaziyo lwayo namaxwebhu onke
akule ndawo yokugcina.

Ipapashwe phantsi kwelayisensi ethi **Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.

<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>

## Unelungelo

- **Lokwabelana** — ukukopa nokusasaza lo msebenzi nangayiphi na
  indlela nakuyiphi na imo, ngayo nayiphi na injongo, kuquka
  urhwebo.
- **Lokulungelelanisa** — ukuxuba, ukutshintsha nokwakha phezu
  kwalo msebenzi.

## Phantsi kwale miqathango

- **Ukunika imbeko (Attribution)** — chaza umthombo (**the Selah
  project**, https://selahproject.com), nika ikhonkco lelayisensi,
  kwaye uchaze ukuba wenze utshintsho na.
- **Ukwabelana ngendlela efanayo (ShareAlike)** — sasaza umsebenzi
  wakho ophuma koku phantsi kwelayisensi efanayo.

Umbhalo osemthethweni welayisensi ngulowo ukwikhonkco elingentla.

## Umbhalo wesiHebhere

Umbhalo wesiHebhere osisiseko (OSHB / WLC 4.20) ukwindawo kawonke-wonke
(public domain). Le layisensi isebenza **kuphela** kwinguqulelo,
kwiinkcazelo, kwiimpawu nakwimbali yohlaziyo — ezo zinto zezeprojekthi
yeSelah.

## Ngokucacileyo

Le nguqulelo **linqina**, hayi ilizwi lokugqibela. Yenziwe ngomatshini,
ilandela uqeqesho olubhaliweyo. Oyisasazayo makayisasaze ekwazi oko.

Yifunde ecaleni kwesiHebhere.
